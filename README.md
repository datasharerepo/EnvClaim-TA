# EnvClaim-TA
Topic-Aligned Extraction and Classification of Environmental Claims from News - Dataset

This is the GitHub repository for the paper "Topic-Aligned Extraction and Classification of Environmental Claims from News". Here, you will find the sample for datasets created in the paper. The paper itself delivers a detailed description of the dataset creation. Complete data will be available once paper is accepted

Dataset Description: 

News Article Link: Url of News Article

News Headline: Headline of News Article

Sentence: Potential Claim Sentences

Topics of Claim: Topics present in Claim sentence

Topic. Sentiment: Sentiment of Topics

Label: Classification of sentence
